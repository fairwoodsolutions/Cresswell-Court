What is 3 Cresswell Court?

3 Cresswell Court is a luxury first floor apartment set within the historic town walls, in the centre of Tenby. Fully refurbished and finished with quality fittings, it comes with free wifi and its own private off-road parking, a rare thing in the old town.

Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know before you book. For anything else, send us a request above.

    

      
How do I book a stay at Cresswell Court?
Choose your dates in the calendar and send us the request. We check availability, reply the same day and send a secure card payment link. Please note that your dates are not secured until payment is received: until then they remain live and available to other guests, including on Airbnb, so please pay promptly once you have the link.

      
Which days can I arrive and leave?
Arrival is on a Monday or a Friday, and the minimum stay is 3 nights. Check-in is from 3.00pm and check-out is strictly by 10.00am. Departure is on a Monday, Thursday or Friday. Christmas week (21 to 29 December 2026) is the exception: you can arrive and leave on any day, with a 3 night minimum. A Monday arrival can leave on Thursday, Friday or the following Monday, and a Friday arrival on Monday, Thursday or Friday.

      
Is there parking at the apartment?
Yes. The apartment has its own private off-road parking space right outside the entrance, which is rare inside Tenby's old town walls.

      
Who can stay at the apartment?
The apartment is available to couples and single guests only, up to 2 guests, and the lead booker must be over 25. It is on the first floor with its own staircase, so it may not suit anyone with limited mobility. Ask us if you are unsure.

      
Are pets allowed?
No. The hosts do not accept pets.

      
Payment and deposit
Your booking is with Lloyd & Paula Jones, trading as Fairwood Properties, and payment goes to Fairwood Properties. Full payment is taken by card through a secure Stripe link when we confirm your dates. Where we think it necessary, for example for a first-time guest, we may ask for a refundable damage deposit of . If we do, we will tell you before you arrive, and it is returned within 7 days of departure if there is no damage.

      
Cancellation
  • 14 days or more before arrival: full refund.
  • 7 to 13 days before arrival: 50% refund.
  • Fewer than 7 days: no refund.
To cancel, reply to your confirmation email. Refunds are paid to the original card. The full terms are in our Holiday Let Agreement.

      
Booking direct or through Airbnb
A direct booking is a contract between you and us. It is not covered by Airbnb's guest protections or AirCover. If you prefer that protection, you are welcome to book through the Airbnb listing at the usual price.

      
Where exactly is the apartment?
3 Cresswell Court, Cresswell Street, Tenby SA70 7HG. It is a first floor apartment inside the historic town walls, a stone's throw from Castle Beach and close to the harbour.

      
How is my data used?
We keep your name, contact details and stay dates in a private booking record, used only to handle your booking, take payment and meet our legal and tax duties. If you tick the optional box, we also email you about availability. You can ask us to delete or correct your details at any time. Full details are in our privacy notice.
